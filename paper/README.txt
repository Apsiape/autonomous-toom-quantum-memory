An autonomous four-dimensional quantum memory from a time-independent Toom Lindbladian
Seth Douglas and Nidhal Mghirbi

abstract.txt is the abstract of main.tex as plain text.

Build main.tex with pdfLaTeX, run BibTeX on main, then pdfLaTeX twice more, without shell escape. main.bbl is included, so pdfLaTeX alone (three passes) also works. The bibliography source is references.bib; no figures or external files are needed. The resulting main.pdf is the compiled preprint.
